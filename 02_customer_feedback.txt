TITLE: Siam Corner Cafe — Local Customer Feedback
DOCUMENT TYPE: Sample survey summary
DATA STATUS: FICTIONAL teaching dataset; not collected from real customers.
LOCATION CONTEXT: Hypothetical Bangkok cafe customers.
PERIOD: September 2026.

Forty-five customers completed a short optional feedback survey in September 2026. The survey is a small convenience sample; it may not be representative of all customers. Survey respondents could choose multiple suggestions, so counts do not need to add to 45.

22 out of 45 respondents said they wanted shorter waits during the lunch period. 18 respondents said they would like a simple loyalty-stamp program. 15 respondents requested an oat-milk option. 12 respondents were interested in ordering ahead before picking up their drinks.

Illustrative qualitative comments (fictional):
- "I have only a short break around noon and cannot wait too long."
- "I would come back more often if there were a straightforward stamp card."
- "An order-ahead option would be convenient when traveling to work."
- "Please keep prices clear and the ordering process simple."

Possible business questions: Is the 12:00–14:00 queue the most urgent bottleneck? Will reducing queue time improve repeat purchases? Is a low-cost loyalty stamp pilot worth testing? Does demand for oat milk justify a new inventory item?

Limitations: There is no direct willingness-to-pay measurement, no representative sampling, no customer lifetime value estimate, and no proof that suggestions will improve sales. Treat all recommendations as hypotheses to validate.
